What the engine sounds like

Two unedited renders, straight out of Harmonia: a 6-operator FM matrix, a symbolic math DSP virtual machine, and a mastering chain — pure Rust, stdlib only. No samples, no DAW, no cloud synth. Every sound below came from a JSON spec.

Bach Meets Vangelis

Download ↓
0:24FM brassmorphing indexcathedral

Retro-classical FM brass under a modulation index that keeps drifting — the Chowning brass that made FM famous, opened up into a reverb cathedral. The peak sits at −0.7 dBFS because that is the default normalize target, not a limiter rescue.

0:000:24
Make it yourself
harmonia render bach_meets_vangelis --sr 96000 --bits 32f -o master.wav

Neon Organum

Download ↓
0:25organ paddense harmonicsshimmer

An organ-style pad stack built from a dense harmonic series — no sampled pipe organ, just sine operators tuned into a chord and fed through shimmer plus the cathedral tail. This is the sound the sustained-pad pattern was written for.

0:000:25
Make it yourself
harmonia render neon_organum --tuning just -o master.wav
